# Strategy

**Who:** startup founders and small business owners in India who sign NDAs and vendor contracts. Tenants are phase 2.

**Problem:** a lawyer's review costs time and money, and most people sign without understanding the contract.

**Why us:**

- Answers cite the exact clause.
- Blank fields are flagged instead of invented.
- No document text goes to analytics.
- Answer quality is tested: 18/20 on my [eval](../04-evals/eval-summary.md).

**Not yet:**

- Lawyers or enterprises as customers.
- Legal advice.
- Payments, until users reliably get their first answer.

How we reach these users: [GTM and onboarding plan](../09-gtm/gtm-onboarding-plan.md).
