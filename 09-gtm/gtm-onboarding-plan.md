# Go-to-Market and Onboarding Plan

See also: [strategy](../01-case-study/strategy.md).

## 1. Who we target

**Phase 1:** startup founders and small business owners in India who sign NDAs, vendor contracts and hiring contracts.

**Phase 2:** tenants with rental agreements, reached through WhatsApp and tenant Facebook groups. We follow each group's rules and do not spam.

**Why founders first:**

- They sign contracts often.
- They care about privacy.
- They are more likely to pay later.
- They are reachable on LinkedIn, where I already post.

## 2. Message

> Before you sign an NDA or vendor contract, get a plain-English review with exact clause citations. Your documents are never sent to analytics and can be deleted anytime. Our AI provider, Cloudflare, states it does not use customer inputs or outputs to train AI models without consent.

## 3. Channels

| Channel | How often |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn posts | 1 per week |
| Direct messages to founders | Ongoing |

## 4. Onboarding

Sign up → upload → see analysis → ask one question.

**Success metric:** new users who ask their first question.

## 5. Targets

| Target | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| 10 founders try it, and 5 of them ask a question | 2 weeks |
| 100 users (stretch) | 6 weeks |

**Switch rule:** if founders don't convert within 2 weeks, switch to tenants.

## 6. Budget

None until the first-question rate improves.

## 7. Risks

- **Small sample size:** a handful of users can't show a reliable rate.
- **Mobile preview:** shows the internal document ID instead of a readable preview (see [UAT](../08-testing/uat-test-cases.md)).
- **Any PDF is accepted,** even non-legal ones.
